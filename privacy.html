Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

At Friendly Surfaces, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website or contact us regarding our products and catalogues.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is primarily designed to showcase our products and catalogues. We do not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the website.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, you may share information such as your name, phone number, messages and product enquiry details. We use this information to respond to your enquiry.

2. WhatsApp Communication

Our website provides WhatsApp links for customer enquiries. When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is handled through WhatsApp, a service provided by Meta.

WhatsApp processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Please review WhatsApp's privacy policy for more details.

WhatsApp Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Information

Information shared with us through enquiries may be used to:

  • Respond to customer questions.
  • Provide product and catalogue information.
  • Understand your requirements.
  • Communicate about your enquiry.

4. Payments and Customer Accounts

Currently, this website does not provide online payments, shopping cart, checkout facilities or customer account registration. Product enquiries are handled through WhatsApp.

5. Cookies and Website Data

We do not intentionally collect personal information through website forms or account registration. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as server logs when visitors access the website. Such information is subject to the hosting provider's applicable policies.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services. Please review their respective policies.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. However, no internet communication or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or business practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your enquiry information is handled, please contact Friendly Surfaces through our Contact Us page or WhatsApp.
