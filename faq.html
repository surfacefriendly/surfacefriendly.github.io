NEED A LITTLE HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Open a question to read the answer. If you need details about a particular product, you can ask us directly on WhatsApp.

What products can I explore on Friendly Surfaces?

You can explore plywood, laminates (including mica) and decorative surface options shown in the website's product and catalogue sections. The available catalogues provide more details about the ranges you can browse.

Do you handle retail as well as wholesale enquiries?

Yes. Friendly Surfaces welcomes both retail and wholesale enquiries. Share your requirements with us so we can guide you on the next steps.

How can I check the price of a product?

Send us the product name or relevant catalogue/design through WhatsApp and ask for current pricing. Prices are not confirmed on this FAQ page.

How do I check whether a product is available?

Availability can vary by product. Contact us with the product name, code (if shown) or catalogue reference so we can respond to your enquiry.

Can I view product designs before enquiring?

Yes. Browse the website's product section and open the available PDF catalogues to review designs before sending your enquiry.

How do I place an enquiry?

Use a WhatsApp enquiry link on the website or the contact page. Include the product name or design you are interested in and any questions you have.

Can I buy directly through the website?

The website is designed for product browsing and enquiries, not online checkout or online payment. Contact us to discuss the product you need.

Where is Friendly Surfaces located?

Business address details have not yet been added to the website. Please contact us on WhatsApp for enquiries until the address is published here.

What should I include in my WhatsApp message?

Include the product name, design or code if available, whether your enquiry is retail or wholesale, and any details you would like to confirm, such as price or availability.
